# A new way to claim expenses

You will no longer fill in a paper form to get paid back for what you spend on work.
You will use an app instead. The app is called ACME Expenses.

## What you need to do

1. Look for the green receipt icon on your work phone after Monday, March 4, 2030.
2. Open it and sign in with your usual work password.
3. Check that the account you get paid back into is right.
4. Do this by Friday, March 15, 2030. It takes about 2 minutes.

After that, you take a photo of each receipt and the app fills in the rest.

## When the app is down

You cannot send a claim from 10 PM Saturday, March 16, 2030, to 2 AM Sunday,
March 17, 2030, local time. Email and chat keep working.

## What stays the same

The same manager approves your claims. You get paid back on the same schedule.

## Help

Go to help.acme.example/expenses. The page also lets you ask the service desk.
