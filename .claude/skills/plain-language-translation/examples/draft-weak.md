# Expense management modernization

As part of our ongoing commitment to efficiency, the organization is deploying an ERP expense solution which will provide a seamless transition away from legacy paper-based claims processing for all personnel across every department.

Users will be required to complete onboarding, which will empower them to submit claims through OCR-enabled receipt capture. Onboarding should be completed in the coming weeks. The legacy form will be decommissioned shortly. Minimal impact is expected. Please reach out to IT with questions.
