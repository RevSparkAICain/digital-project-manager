# House style

This skill holds the writing standard for your organization's work done in digital-project-manager. It
applies to Chief of Staff and to every specialist agent, in every output a person
reads.

The full standard, including the editable lists, is in
[writing-standard.md](writing-standard.md). Read it before drafting anything longer than a
few sentences, and again before you hand a draft back.

## The rules in short

1. **Answer first.** The first sentence carries the answer, the recommendation, or the
   decision needed. No opener (except a by-name greeting on the first reply of a session,
   if the workspace uses one), no throat-clearing, no closing summary that repeats what
   was already said.
2. **No banned phrases.** Stock phrases that add length without content are listed in the
   standard. Close variants count.
3. **No banned words.** Each banned word has a plain swap listed next to it. Use the swap,
   or say the specific thing the word was standing in for.
4. **No em dashes.** Use a colon, a comma, parentheses, or a period.
5. **Meet the quality bar.** Be complete and specific, keep a professional structure, and
   report what a source says without reinterpreting it.

## How to apply it

- **While drafting:** write the answer sentence first, then the supporting detail in the
  order the reader needs it.
- **Before handing back:** scan the draft against both lists and for the em dash
  character. Fix every hit rather than reporting it.
- **When reviewing someone else's draft:** flag list hits and em dashes as formatting
  fixes. Treat wording changes beyond the lists as suggestions for the author to accept.
- **Technical terms are exempt.** A literal command name, a metric name, a product name,
  or a statistical term keeps its real spelling even when it matches a banned word. The
  lists target prose, not accuracy.

## Changing the standard

The lists belong to the team that runs this workspace, and the team edits them. To add,
remove, or reword an entry, change `writing-standard.md`. Keep each banned word paired with a
plain swap so a writer always knows what to use instead.

## Enforcement

The pre-handback checklist in section 6 of `writing-standard.md` is the control.
