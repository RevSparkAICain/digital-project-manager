# Alt text for the example assets

Each entry is the text a screen reader speaks in place of the image. It states the message
and the action, not the file name.

## infographic

A four-step guide titled "Scanner app update: what you do before Monday, March 18, 2030".
The steps are: pick up your scanner from the charging rack, open the inventory app and tap
Update, sign in with your usual badge and PIN, and scan one test label to confirm. It takes
about 3 minutes and must be done before your first pick on March 18. For help, open an
"Inventory app" request with the ACME IT Service Desk.
