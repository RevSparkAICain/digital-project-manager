# Cascade packet: purchase approvals move to a web form

Tier: standard. Prepared for the Finance and Operations leaders. Go-live: Monday, June 3, 2030.

## Manager email

Subject: Your team approves purchases on a new form from June 3

Hello,

From Monday, June 3, 2030, purchase approvals move from email to a web form. Your team
will no longer approve by replying to a message.

Anyone on your team who approves spending is affected. They must sign in to the form once
and set a backup approver before the change.

Your actions are listed below.

1. Present the talking points when your team next meets. Due: Friday, May 24, 2030.
2. Send the blurb on to people who missed that meeting. Due: Monday, May 27, 2030.
3. Give us the names of approvers who cannot sign in. Due: Wednesday, May 29, 2030.

The first IT Bulletin goes to all staff on Monday, May 20, 2030. A reminder follows on
Monday, May 27, 2030.

If you see a problem with this plan, tell me by Wednesday, May 15, 2030.

Jane Doe, Project Lead

## Talking points

- "On June 3 you will approve purchases on a web form, not by email."
  - If someone asks: "Will I still get a message?" Yes. You get a short note with a link to the form.
- "Set up your backup approver before June 3. It takes about two minutes."
  - If someone asks: "Who picks my backup?" You do. Choose someone who can approve the same amounts.
- "Approvals made by email after June 3 will not count."
  - If someone asks: "What about an urgent one?" Use the form on your phone. It works anywhere.
- "If you cannot sign in, tell me by Wednesday, May 29, and we will fix it."
  - If someone asks: "Who fixes it?" The IT Service Desk, and I will pass your name along.

## FAQ

**Why are we doing this?**

Email approvals get lost. The form keeps every approval in one place, so nothing sits in an inbox. Leadership made the decision and it is not optional.

**How long will this take me?**

About two minutes to sign in and pick a backup. After that, each approval takes less time than an email reply.

**What happens if I do nothing?**

On June 3 you will not be able to approve purchases. Orders waiting on you will stop until you set up.

**We just changed this. Why again?**

The last change moved requests. This one moves approvals, so the two now match.

**My device will not run it. What now?**

Tell me and the IT Service Desk. The form runs in any modern browser, including on a phone.

**Who do I call when it breaks?**

Use the IT Service Desk help page named in the bulletin. I can also pass on your question.

**Can this be undone if it goes badly?**

Yes. The IT team can switch back to email approvals while a problem is fixed, and we would tell you the same day.

## Team meeting script

1. Opening, 1 minute. Say: "From June 3, we approve purchases on a web form instead of email."
2. Points and questions, 3 minutes. Read the four talking points, then answer from the FAQ above. Write down anything you cannot answer.
3. Close, 1 minute. Say: "Please sign in and pick a backup by May 29. Tell me if you cannot."
