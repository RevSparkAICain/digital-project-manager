Subject: [IT Bulletin] Planned change: Printers need your badge from March 4

## At a glance

From Monday, March 4, 2030, shared printers will hold your pages until you tap your ACME
badge on the printer. Link your badge by Friday, March 1, 2030.

## Who is affected

Everyone who uses a shared printer at an ACME office or warehouse. Shift teams print the
most, so they will notice it the most. Label printers on the warehouse floor do not change.

## What you need to do

1. Link your badge by Friday, March 1, 2030. Tap it on the reader on the front of any
   shared printer, then type your employee number. It takes about 1 minute.
2. Start using your badge on Monday, March 4, 2030. Print as you do today. Then tap your
   badge on any shared printer to get your pages.

## What to expect

Shared printers will be off on Sunday, March 3, 2030, from 6 PM to 10 PM local time. The IT
team will set them up then.

Each shared printer now has a small badge reader on the front. The screen says "Tap your
badge" until you do. You can get your pages at any shared printer, not just the one you
sent them to.

Your pages wait in the printer for 24 hours. After that, they are deleted. Pages no longer
sit in the tray for anyone to pick up.

You still print from your computer the same way. Printer names stay the same.

## Get help

Go to the [printing help page](https://help.acme.example/printing). You can also ask the IT
Service Desk for help from that page.
